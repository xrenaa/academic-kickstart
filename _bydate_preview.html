Xuanchi Ren

I am a senior research scientist at the NVIDIA Spatial Intelligence Lab. I am also a Ph.D. student at the University of Toronto, advised by Prof. Sanja Fidler. During my undergraduate years at HKUST, I was supervised by Prof. Qifeng Chen, and I was fortunate to work with Dr. Li Erran Li, Prof. Alexandre Alahi (EPFL), Dr. Yuwang Wang (MSRA) and Prof. Xiaolong Wang (UCSD).

My research interests are in computer vision, particularly generative modeling and 3D vision. I am interested in building world models and 3D generative models for physical AI, with a focus on scalable representations, controllability and 3D consistency.
